About the App

Every idea can become a source of income. SparkScale helps you track the progress of your side projects and see how your efforts translate into results.

Many people start side projects but lose motivation due to a lack of visible progress. SparkScale shows real numbers: how much you've earned, how many hours you've invested, and which project is most profitable. This helps you make decisions based on data, not assumptions.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2023

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SparkScale ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information Collection

SparkScale is designed to be privacy-first. The data regarding your projects, earnings, and hours worked is stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

2. Data Usage

Any data entered into the app is used solely for the purpose of providing you with analytics and insights about your projects. We do not have access to your financial inputs unless you explicitly share them with us for support purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use standard Apple services (like iCloud) to sync your data across your devices if you enable such features. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for data stored in iCloud.
